Option D shrinks to reporting fixes and one new input.**
2. **The wrong numbers are still wrong**: `adjust_both_groups` credits reference raises to the compared group (REM-3: +2 103 reported, −434 true); the app's reduction % is now in range but non-monotone (100 % at a 56 472 $ cap, 58 % at 80 000 $); `check_defensibility` prints every gap with the opposite sign (REM-5); a schedule that overpays everyone by 3× is 99/99 "defensible" (REM-6); `target_gap` is read nowhere (REM-7); negative and NaN budgets fund the full need.
3. **Two things are gone**: the throwaway bootstrap (PERF-3) and the frontier/interval normal-vs-t and pooled-design defects (REM-9 b, c).
4. **One new finding with a client-visible figure**: on the 10 000-row employers fixture "Budget complet" costs 33 231 528 $ and leaves the compared group 6 491 $ *above* the line, while bringing the group's mean gap to zero would cost 1 475 043 $ (22.5×). The screen never says the remedy is per person, not per group. That is the real content of `target_gap`.
5. **Founder decisions: six**, all on what the consultant and the employer see (§ 2). Everything else is decided in § 3.
6. **Cost of the fix**: additive engine fields, one arithmetic correction, copy, tests that can fail. No new dependency, no WASM growth, no change to any amount on clean data with the toggle off.

## 2. Founder decisions

Only choices that change what a client or regulator sees or what the remedy promises. Recommended option first. Each option: one sentence on what the consultant sees.

**D1. What does « combler l'écart » promise on the Remediation step: every compared person to their line, or the group's gap to a figure?**
1. **Both, named (recommended).** The step keeps today's amounts (every compared employee below the line is raised to it; 33.2 M$ on the employers roster) and gains one input « Ramener l'écart de groupe à X $ » that derives the budget (1.48 M$ for X = 0 on the same roster), says in one line when X cannot be reached and what the lowest reachable gap is, and the tiles name which of the two the amounts close.
2. **Per-person only.** The step says « chaque personne sous la courbe est ramenée à sa courbe », the target-gap field is deleted from the type and the MCP schema, and the consultant has no way to cost a group-level closure.
3. **Remedy defined on the post-adjustment group coefficient (a real LP, clarabel or an efficiency greedy).** The consultant sees a cheaper schedule that pays the employees whose raise moves the pooled coefficient most per dollar, so who is paid changes and an employer or a CNESST reviewer cannot read the order as fairness.
4. **Statutory mode.** The step computes one adjustment per female-predominant job class paid to every employee of the class in at most four equal annual instalments (arts 60-70), the slider becomes a number of instalments, and the regression remedy gets its own separate name; a product change that crosses into the legislative module.

**D2. What is the percentage beside the budget?**
1. **« Part du besoin financée » = cost_T / need_T under the current threshold (recommended).** Reads 0-100 %, rises with every dollar, 100 % when every eligible shortfall is paid, 24.8 % when 20 000 $ of 80 696 $ is paid, the same for both strategies at the same cost, and no figure when the need is zero.
2. **Same ratio with the full shortfall as denominator** (every compared row below the line, threshold ignored): the figure caps below 100 % whenever a gap threshold excludes rows (79.5 % at full budget with a 5 % threshold), which is the honest reading for a reviewer and a surprise for the consultant who set the threshold.
3. **Keep today's mean-gap ratio clamped to 0-100.** Moving the cap from 56 472 $ to 80 000 $ lowers the figure from 100 % to 58 % while the cost rises.
4. **No percentage.** « 20 000 $ payés sur un besoin de 80 696 $ », « 60 696 $ restent à payer », and VERIFY's remaining gap in dollars with its direction in words; the scenario comparison table loses its sortable column.

**D3. « Inclure le groupe de référence » (adjust_both_groups): what does the employer see?**
1. **Fix the arithmetic and show the reference raises as their own cost line (recommended).** New gap and remaining gap become the true post-remedy values (−434 $, not +2 103 $ on the probe roster); the tile reads « 80 696 $ pour le groupe comparé, 59 905 $ pour le groupe de référence »; a sentence says the baseline line moves when reference employees are raised; the percentage counts compared-group payments only.
2. **Remove the box from the screen**, keep the field for MCP callers with the corrected arithmetic; the consultant can no longer raise reference employees from the Remediation step.
3. **Keep it as is.** The saved-scenario table and the reduction percentage keep crediting reference raises to the compared group's gap.

**D4. After a schedule is applied, what does the employer read about employees who land above their range, and about the group?**
1. **Position in words, with counts, and the exact group test (recommended).** « 30 personnes dans la fourchette, 0 sous, 9 au-dessus (déjà au-dessus avant les ajustements) », and « après ajustement, le coefficient de groupe est +104 $ (t = 3,5, p = 0,0006), le groupe comparé se trouve au-dessus de la courbe »; « défendable » stays a per-row floor and never a group verdict; reference rows are not counted.
2. **Keep « défendable » one-sided** (at or above the lower bound passes): a schedule that overpays everyone by 3× still reads 100 % defensible, and the group line stays the bootstrap p from VERIFY.
3. **Treat above-the-range as a failure.** Red marks on rows the Act forbids cutting (art. 73), so the employer reads a problem the remedy cannot fix.

**D5. What is a capped budget, in the words the employer reads?**
1. **A scenario (recommended).** « Scénario à 40 000 $ : 49,6 % du besoin financé; 23 personnes restent sous leur courbe; 40 696 $ non financés »; the Act is kept off the tile; the PDF methodology block states once that the Act spreads adjustments over at most four equal annual payments (art. 70) and that a scenario is not that schedule.
2. **A first instalment.** The tile adds « solde dû : 40 696 $ » and the PDF presents the cap as instalment 1 of k ≤ 4 (art. 70), which is only true when the cap equals the need divided by k.
3. **A closure.** Today's reading: the cap is the remedy and nothing names what was left unpaid.

**D6. The frontier chart beside a Pooled or Equitable scenario**
1. **The chart follows the screen's remedy settings (recommended, same epic).** The curve and its « premier budget où l'écart ne se distingue plus de zéro » point are computed with the consultant's target, strategy, range and threshold, and the budget axis ends at that remedy's need.
2. **Caption the chart with the remedy it describes** (« Groupe de référence, les plus grands écarts d'abord ») and defer the request change; the consultant sees a curve for another remedy, labelled.
3. **Hide the chart when the screen is not Reference / Greedy.**
