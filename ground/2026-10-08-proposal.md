## Option A — Every remedy dollar lands on the right employee  [type: carried-debt]
Problem: the optimiser pairs dollar amounts to employees by raw-file position while computing the amounts on a frame with blank-cell rows removed. One blank cell in any model column, or a third value in the gender column, shifts every later pairing; the last employee is never paid. `check_defensibility` crashes on the same shapes. Known since 0017 P4, deliberately not repaired, pinned by a test.
Evidence: BND-1, REM-1 (critical, 2 of 2 skeptics); REM-2, REM-4, BND-2 (high); BND-11, CMP-3 (rows-analysed reporting). `engine/src/analysis.rs:452-462` vs `:477-479`; `engine/src/verification_test.rs:511-556`; app pre-flight `analysisResults.store.js:29`, `:123-130`. Probe: one blank cell in 40 target rows -> 36 of 39 paid rows land more than $1 off their own fair wage; verify of the same schedule leaves -152.92 unexplained where the optimiser claimed 0.00.
Expected observable delta: on the probe roster (one blank cell; and a third gender value) every paid row lands on its own fair wage to the cent and verify agrees with the optimiser; `check_defensibility` returns a named error or a result, never a trap; the app refuses or names blank model cells and extra group values before the run; every result carries rows used and rows dropped.
What would refute it: the probe still shows any row landing off its fair wage after the fix; or the app has a path that reaches OPTIMIZE without the pre-flight.
Appetite: scoped (engine + app pre-flight + WASM rebuild/publish + verify-live receipt in the app)
Blast radius: none Tier-4. Cross-repo: engine change, then `scripts/build-wasm.sh` publishes into `pay-equity-app`; the app's `verify-live.sh` is the receipt. The test that pins the wrong pairing is replaced, not deleted.

## Option C — The numbers a consultant reads as findings are statistically sound  [type: statistical correctness]
Problem: several outputs look like findings and are artefacts. Per-category unexplained "drivers" change with the omitted base category; a dataset with no gender effect but non-overlapping education reports a significant unexplained gap with no warning; fractional (FTE) weights move the weighted median; the quantile "total gap" is a mean-RIF difference; the app's default Pooled scheme has no external oracle.
Evidence: MEAN-1 (high, 2/2), CMP-1 (critic, reproduced with the CLI: true unexplained 0, reported -3116, p=0.02), QRI-1 (high, 2/2), QRI-2 (high), BND-3 / TRUST-8 (Pooled unverified), MEAN-2, MEAN-3, MEAN-4, QRI-5.
Expected observable delta: detailed categorical drivers are normalised (Yun / Gardeazabal-Ugidos) on the shipped surfaces or not presented as findings; a covariate-support and residual-df diagnostic appears on the result and the no-overlap golden triggers it; weighted quantiles are exact for fractional weights; Pooled two-fold pinned to R `oaxaca` at 1e-6.
What would refute it: an R/Stata oracle reproduces the current per-dummy numbers as the conventional reported quantity; or the support diagnostic fires on ordinary balanced rosters (false-positive rate measured on the existing goldens).
Appetite: balanced (statistics specs drafted with Fable, built on Sonnet, each change pinned to an external oracle)
Blast radius: changes numbers users see; app copy for drivers and the diagnostic needs a design pass.

## Option D — The remedy does what it says  [type: new-capability + wrong-number]
Problem: "optimisation" is a sort-and-cap heuristic that ignores `target_gap`; `adjust_both_groups` credits reference-group raises to the target gap; the headline reduction can read over 100%; the frontier and intervals ignore the user's settings; the defensibility flag is a per-employee prediction floor described as group evidence; the remedy tests cannot fail on the property they name.
Evidence: REM-7, REM-3, REM-8, REM-9, REM-6, REM-5, TRUST-9, PERF-3.
Expected observable delta: a convex solve (clarabel is already a dependency) that meets `target_gap` at minimum cost or reports infeasibility; closure arithmetic that verify reproduces for every strategy; tests that go red when the remedy pays the wrong group or misses the target.
What would refute it: the sort-and-cap result is already cost-optimal for the objective the app actually states (then D shrinks to the reporting fixes).
Appetite: balanced
Blast radius: changes remedy amounts for some configurations; needs A first (a solver fed misaligned rows is still wrong).

## Recommendation
A, because it optimises **no employee is paid from another employee's numbers**: it is the only finding where real money reaches the wrong person, it is reachable with one blank cell, and every other option builds on correct row pairing. If you want **trustworthy signals before more change**, pick B first; it is the floor under every later epic. Proposed order after A: B, C, D, then a consolidation cycle.
